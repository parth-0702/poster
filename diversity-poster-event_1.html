Rules & Regulations

Guidelines for Participants 📋

  • 01 Topic is fixed: Your poster must be based on "Diversity in India". No other theme will be accepted.
  • 02 Time limit: You will get exactly 1 hour and 15 minutes. Work must stop when time is called.
  • 03 Materials: Use only the colours, brushes, sheets, pencils, erasers, and scale provided by the organizers.
  • 04 Internet for ideas only: You may use the internet to search for inspiration and reference images.
  • 05 Originality is mandatory: Your poster must show your own creativity and artistic expression.
  • 06 No copying: Do not directly copy or reproduce any complete poster design found online. This will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • 07 Neat presentation: Label your poster with your name and participant ID before submission.
  • 08 Respect & inclusivity: Your poster must reflect positive and respectful representation of all communities.
⚠️ Disqualification Warning
Copying any poster entirely from the internet or reproducing another participant's work will result in immediate disqualification from the event. Your creativity is your superpower — use it!
